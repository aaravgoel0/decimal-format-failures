# Secondary scale-control deviation record

Recorded after completion of the three-model primary study.

The frozen numerical-invariance protocol specified a feasibility gate for the
secondary checkpoint `mlx-community/Qwen3-30B-A3B-Instruct-2507-4bit`. This
secondary control was not executed. A hardware-safe loading configuration was
not established in the local execution environment, and the author chose not
to risk system instability after repeated memory pressure during the primary
Gemma runs.

No prompt from either new dataset was evaluated with the 30B checkpoint, no
30B output was inspected, and no analysis or manuscript claim depends on this
secondary control. The three native-precision checkpoints specified as the
primary models were completed under the documented complete-base rules.

This record documents a deviation from the frozen protocol rather than
retroactively changing the protocol or presenting an unexecuted control as a
result.
